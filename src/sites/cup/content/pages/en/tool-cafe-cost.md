## What it calculates

For a single cup it works out the variable cost (coffee, milk, packaging, extras), the net price after tax and the margin that is left. It then multiplies the margin by your volume to show the monthly contribution towards rent, staff and other fixed costs.

## The formulas

- **Net price** = selling price ÷ (1 + tax rate)
- **Coffee cost** = price per kg × grams per cup ÷ 1000
- **Milk cost** = millilitres per cup ÷ 1000 × price per litre
- **Margin per cup** = net price − (coffee + milk + packaging + other)
- **Monthly contribution** = margin per cup × cups per day × opening days

## Reading the result

Contribution is not profit: rent, wages, energy, equipment and insurance are still paid out of it. Use it to compare prices, recipes and suppliers. For example: how much does a different bean change your margin per cup?

## Useful checks

- Try two selling prices one after the other to see the effect on your monthly contribution.
- For takeaway, include cup, lid and sleeve in packaging.
- Check the tax rate that applies to your business: it can differ between eat-in and takeaway, and from country to country.

## FAQ

**Why is the margin calculated on the net price?**
Because the tax part of the price is not revenue for your business: you collect it for the tax authority.
