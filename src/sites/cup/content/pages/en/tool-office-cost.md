## How it works

Enter your team size and how much coffee people drink, then describe two options, each with a cost per cup and a fixed monthly cost (rental, lease, service contract). You get the monthly and yearly cost of each option, the cost per employee, which one is cheaper and the volume at which both cost the same.

## The formulas

- **Cups per month** = employees × coffees per person per day × working days
- **Monthly cost** = cups per month × cost per cup + fixed monthly cost
- **Break-even volume** = difference in fixed costs ÷ difference in cost per cup

## Finding the cost per cup

- **Capsules and pods:** price of the pack ÷ number of capsules.
- **Bean machines:** price per kg × grams per cup ÷ 1000, plus milk if used.
- **Vending or service contracts:** ask the supplier for the all-in price per cup.

## What goes into fixed costs

Machine rental or lease, the service contract, water filters, cleaning products and, for machines you buy, the price spread over the months you expect to use them.

## FAQ

**Is a bean-to-cup machine always cheaper?**
Not always: its lower cost per cup has to pay back a higher fixed cost. The break-even volume shows where it starts to pay off.
