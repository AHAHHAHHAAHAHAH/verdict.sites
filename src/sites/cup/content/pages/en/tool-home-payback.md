## What the calculator does

It compares the cost of making a drink at home with the price you pay for the same drink out. Then it tells you how long the difference takes to cover the price of the machine.

## The formulas

- **Cost of a drink at home** = price of beans per kg × grams per drink ÷ 1000 + other costs per drink
- **Saving per drink** = price out − cost at home
- **Net saving per year** = saving per drink × drinks per day × days per week × 52 − maintenance per year
- **Payback time** = machine price ÷ net saving per day

## How to find your own numbers

- **Beans:** divide the price of the bag by its weight in kilograms. For example, a 250 g bag for 6 costs 24 per kg.
- **Grams per drink:** check your machine's manual or weigh a dose. Single and double shots use different amounts.
- **Maintenance:** add up descaler, water filters and cleaning tablets over a year.

## What is not included

Electricity, your time, the resale value of the machine, and the chance that you drink more coffee once you have a machine at home. If they matter to you, add them to "other cost per drink" or to maintenance.

## FAQ

**Does a cheaper machine always pay for itself sooner?**
Only if it makes the drinks you actually want. A machine you stop using never pays for itself.

**What if I only replace some of my coffees out?**
Enter only the drinks you would really stop buying out.
